A little context

Research roots, practical habits.

I started in medical research. The habits from it travel well: treat an idea as a claim, and ask what would change your mind. These days I work across data science and product at {{ $profile.employer }}, building tools for people who know more about the problem than the software does.

This site is the longer version: what I have built, what I am testing, and the questions that keep outliving the tools. If you are working through something similar, especially if the first attempt did not go the way you expected, I would like to hear about it.
