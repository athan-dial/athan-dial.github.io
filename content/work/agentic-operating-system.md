This is a map of how I work with AI agents. It is a personal operating system for agent-enabled work, not a company product, and it makes no claim that any organization runs this way.

## Expert judgment stays at the center. Agents carry the work.

[The Outer Loop](/essays/the-outer-loop/) argues that a faster plan-build-verify loop makes it easier to perfect the wrong thing. This atlas is the concrete companion to that idea. It shows where a person keeps the question, the decision and the verdict, and where agents do the reading, drafting, building and checking.

The map has three stages, Thinking, Making and Sharing, joined in a loop: what is learned at the end feeds the next question. Three supporting surfaces sit underneath all of them: knowledge and memory, governance and orchestration, and the execution substrate that runs the agents. Three of the views go deeper where the mechanism matters most: how autonomous work is admitted and verified, where agent autonomy ends, and how context survives from one session to the next.

Each view states its claim, what it is based on and what it does not claim. These are descriptions of a working method as it stands today. They are not an audited runtime, and they leave out older designs that have since been retired.
